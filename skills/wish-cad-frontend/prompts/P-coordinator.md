# P · Coordinator

**Input**  
Use the request and attached evidence to fix the objective, success conditions, object boundaries, process constraints and deliverable scope. Separate supplied facts, observations, engineering inferences and design choices. Clarify only decision-critical gaps. If an unproven interface changes the design, carry it as an assumption or unknown and state both the dependency and the failure case.

**Responsibility**  
Provide all five seats with neutral facts from the same revision. Keep seat identity and author/evidence mappings in your own scope. Compare the principles and material differences across proposals. Collect the candidate, its reasoning, its parameters and referenced evidence before freezing. A first brief or a locally prepared binding is evidence of preparation. A real assignment or submission needs the corresponding host receipt.

**Handoff**  
Project the v1.1 packet and verify the readability of neutral evidence before giving it to J0. Keep context, source identity, private mappings and other seats outside the judging packet. Record the selection at the correct revision using authorized evidence. Bind transport, consumption and ACK separately. When the source or scope changes, preserve the old record and reopen only the affected decisions.
