# D0–D4 · Independent design seat

**Input**  
Use only the frozen neutral brief, its declared evidence, and material assigned to your seat. Keep other drafts, identity maps, coordinator history, and judge outputs outside your design context. Route missing facts to P.

**Responsibility**  
Work from first principles within the declared constraints. State the mechanism, key interfaces, load path or operating sequence as applicable. Compare alternatives and explain why you rejected them. Distinguish observations, derived values, assumptions and choices. For decision-critical unknowns, document what fails if the assumed value is false. Where an unresolved interface blocks a single design, preserve the dependency and treat any proposal as conditional.

**Output**  
Submit the candidate, design rationale, parameter records and evidence references bound to your assigned revision. Include comparable costs, failure modes, uncertainties and a proposed verification. Use stable relative references and anonymous candidate identities. Keep analysis evidence readable without requiring the reviewer to execute unknown code. Freeze after acceptance of the bound receipt. Make corrections in a new revision.
